1 — Exploded assembly

SIDE ELEVATION · NTS
Two printed parts, everything else reused. The adapter bolts flat to the copper plate through two drilled holes; the boss is printed integrally into the enclosure back. Measure the plate before printing — 64 mm centres is a placeholder until you have calipers on it.

2 — Quarter-turn interface

VIEW ON MOUNTING FACE
Why this beats a clamp screw. A tightened joint holds by friction, and engine vibration is very good at walking friction joints loose. Here the lugs sit behind solid shoulders, so load passes through a shear plane instead. The detent stops the twist backing off on rough roads and gives you the click that tells you it's home — the part you'll rely on with gloves on and eyes forward.

3 — Weather sealing

SECTION A–A · ENLARGED
The vent is the part people leave out. Parked in the sun the internal air reaches 60 °C+; hit it with rain and the air contracts, pulling water past the gasket. A Ø3 hole covered with ePTFE from the inside breathes air and blocks liquid. Without it you get condensation on the window within a month no matter how good the seal is.
